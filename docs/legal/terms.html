Last updated: 2026-08-26.

By signing up for AgentVisor AI (the "Service"), you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't sign up.

1. The service

AgentVisor AI is a policy enforcement + audit-receipt layer for AI agents. You install a small daemon in your infrastructure; it enforces the guardrails you configure and posts signed receipts to the hosted control plane at api.agentvisorai.me.

2. Your data

We store the metadata your daemon posts (session summaries, tool invocations, receipt signatures). We do NOT store the plaintext LLM prompts, completions, or tool arguments unless you explicitly opt in via a workspace setting. Password hashes use argon2id. Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (managed provider defaults).

You can export all your data via the console (Settings → Export) at any time. You can delete your account (Settings → Delete Account) which triggers a cascade delete of every deployment, session, event, and receipt in your organization.

3. Acceptable use

Don't use the service to enforce policies on illegal content, spam, or copyright-infringing material. Don't try to break into other tenants' data. Don't try to make the service unavailable to others (i.e., no DoS).
